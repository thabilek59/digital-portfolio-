The details make a difference

Six habits of effective email writing

01

Be specific

Write a subject line that tells the reader what the message concerns instead of using a vague heading.

02

Show respect

Use an appropriate greeting and address the recipient respectfully, especially in academic and workplace settings.

03

Keep it concise

Explain your purpose directly. Organise longer messages into short paragraphs that are easy to read.

04

Use formal language

Avoid unnecessary slang, excessive emojis, and informal abbreviations in professional communication.

05

Proofread carefully

Check names, grammar, spelling, attachments, and recipient addresses before pressing send.

06

Close professionally

Thank the recipient where appropriate and finish with a suitable closing followed by your name and contact details.

Professional etiquette

Small mistakes can distract from a good message.

A quick review before sending helps ensure your message is clear, respectful, and complete.

  • Do not send a message without a meaningful subject line.
  • Do not use all capital letters to express urgency.
  • Do not send unreviewed messages with avoidable spelling errors.
  • Do not forget to attach a document you refer to in the email.
  • Do not use the same generic message for every recipient without adapting it.
