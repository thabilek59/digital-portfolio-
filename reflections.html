01 / LOOKING INWARD

A journey of learning and becoming.

Starting my journey in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics has given me an opportunity to explore how business, technology and data can work together.

As a first-year student at Botswana Accountancy College, I am still building my knowledge and discovering the areas in which I want to grow. I see this stage as an important foundation for my future career.

I am learning to appreciate the process instead of focusing only on the destination. Every new concept, assignment and opportunity to work with others can help me develop academically and personally.

02 / WHAT I'M DISCOVERING

Lessons that are shaping me.

These are some of the lessons I want to carry with me throughout my academic and professional journey.

01

ACADEMIC GROWTH

Learning takes intention.

Developing new knowledge requires curiosity, consistency and a willingness to practise. I want to become more confident in applying what I learn instead of simply memorising it.

02

PERSONAL GROWTH

Progress over perfection.

I do not need to know everything at the beginning. I need to stay open to feedback, recognise opportunities to improve and keep moving forward.

03

COLLABORATION

Growth can be shared.

Working with classmates reminds me that people bring different ideas and perspectives. Listening and contributing can make learning more meaningful.

04

FUTURE READINESS

Small steps build big goals.

My ambitions will require more than motivation. I need to build relevant skills, seek opportunities and turn my interests into practical experience.

03 / ROOM TO GROW

Challenges are part of the becoming.

Studying a degree that connects business, data and technology means developing knowledge across different areas. Some concepts may take extra time and practice to understand, especially while I am still building my technical foundation.

I want to respond to challenges by breaking tasks into manageable steps, practising consistently, asking questions when I need clarification and learning from feedback. I also want to manage my time carefully so that I can balance academic responsibilities with personal development.

Rather than seeing areas I have not mastered as weaknesses that define me, I want to treat them as opportunities for improvement.
