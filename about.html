HELLO, LOVELY SOUL.

A curious mind with a world to explore.

My name is Thabile Khukhwane, and I am a first-year Bachelor of Science in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics student at Botswana Accountancy College.

I am curious, ambitious, free-spirited and independent-minded. I have a deep love for learning and exploring, and I enjoy discovering ideas that challenge the way I think.

I am still discovering all the things I can become, and I see that as one of the most exciting parts of my journey. I want to keep learning, embrace new experiences and build a life that reflects my interests, values and ambitions.

I believe that personal growth and professional success can exist together. For me, education is not just about earning a qualification; it is also about developing the knowledge, confidence and independence to create opportunities for myself.
