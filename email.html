PROFESSIONAL COMMUNICATION · FIRST IMPRESSIONS

Say it with
confidence.

Professional communication is more than sending a message. It is about being clear, respectful and intentional. This is an example of how I would introduce myself when reaching out about a career opportunity.

Read the email ✦ ✧

01 / THE ART OF REACHING OUT

Clear words. Professional energy.

Whether contacting a recruiter, asking about an internship or connecting with someone in an industry, a well-written email can create a positive first impression.

01

Be clear

Use a subject line that tells the recipient exactly what the email is about.

02

Be respectful

Use a suitable greeting and professional language throughout your message.

03

Be intentional

Explain why you are writing and what response or next step you are hoping for.

02 / SAMPLE PROFESSIONAL EMAIL

A message that opens a conversation.

Example: enquiring about an internship or entry-level learning opportunity in data or financial analysis.

PROFESSIONAL MESSAGE

✧
TO

[Recruiter's or organisation's email address]

FROM

Khukhwaneethabilea@gmail.com

SUBJECT

Enquiry About Internship or Entry-Level Opportunities

Dear Hiring Manager,

I hope you are well. My name is Thabile Khukhwane, and I am a first-year Bachelor of Science in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics student at Botswana Accountancy College.

I am writing to enquire whether your organisation offers internship opportunities, student placements or entry-level learning opportunities related to data analysis, financial analysis or business intelligence.

I am interested in how businesses use data and financial information to understand performance and make informed decisions. Through my studies, I am developing foundational skills in computer literacy, Microsoft Office, database management, basic programming and analytical problem-solving.

I would be grateful for any information about suitable opportunities or the process for applying. I would also welcome guidance on the skills and qualifications your organisation values in students pursuing this career direction.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to hearing from you.

Kind regards,
Thabile Khukhwane
BSc Business Intelligence and Data Analytics Student
Botswana Accountancy College
Email: Khukhwaneethabilea@gmail.com
Phone: 72937667

03 / BEFORE YOU PRESS SEND

My professional checklist.

A few simple checks can make a message more effective and help avoid unnecessary mistakes.

✓

Check the recipient

Make sure the email is going to the correct person or organisation.

✓

Review the subject line

Keep it relevant, concise and easy to understand.

✓

Proofread the message

Check spelling, grammar, tone and contact details.

✓

Attach relevant documents

If requested, attach the correct CV or supporting documents before sending.

✓

End professionally

Include a suitable sign-off and accurate contact information.
