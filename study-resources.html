Study smarter

Six strategies for effective learning

These approaches can help organise study time, strengthen understanding, and make challenging subjects feel more manageable.

01

Plan the week

List upcoming classes, assignments, tests, and deadlines. Break larger tasks into smaller steps and schedule time for each one.

02

Practise active recall

Close your notes and explain a concept from memory. Use flashcards or practice questions to discover what you understand and what needs more work.

03

Review over time

Revisit material across several days rather than relying on one long session immediately before an assessment.

04

Practise with data

For analytics and mathematics, work through examples, interpret results, check calculations, and explain what your findings mean.

05

Learn collaboratively

Discuss difficult concepts with classmates, compare approaches, and ask questions. Explain ideas in your own words instead of only copying notes.

06

Protect your focus

Choose a manageable study goal, silence unnecessary notifications, take short breaks, and return to the task with renewed attention.
